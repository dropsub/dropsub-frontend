About DropSub

Company overview

DropSub is an early-stage content monetization platform that enables musicians and their teams to upload, organize, and monetize their creative archives — photos, videos, studio sessions, and tour footage — through tiered fan subscriptions and one-time premium purchases.

Founded in 2025 by Richard Frias, a former manager for international touring artist MIYAVI, DropSub addresses the gap between the content artists create and the content fans can actually access. The company is based in the United States and is currently in early access.

Founded in 2025 by former artist manager Richard Frias, DropSub is currently in early access. Learn more at dropsub.com.

Founder bio

Richard Frias — Founder & CEO

Richard Frias is the founder and CEO of DropSub. He previously managed international touring artist MIYAVI, overseeing content strategy, fan engagement, and digital operations across North America, Europe, and Asia. During that tenure, he identified the gap between the volume of content artists produce and the monetization infrastructure available to them. DropSub is his solution to that problem — built from the inside of artist management, for the teams that live in it.
