How it works

Three steps to monetize
your entire archive

DropSub sits between file storage and premium content subscriptions — giving artists a living, monetized public archive.

Step 01
Upload your archive
Drag and drop your content — photos, videos, audio, documents. Organize into folders by date, event, or type. Your team can contribute directly.
Step 02
Set access tiers
Choose Free to drive discovery, Subscription for recurring revenue, or Premium for one-time exclusive drops. Mix and match across folders.

Fans pay, you earn
Share your DropSub link. Fans subscribe or purchase. Revenue lands directly in your account. Content you already created, earning forever.
